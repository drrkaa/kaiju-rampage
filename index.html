YOUR FIRST RAMPAGE

Small city. Big appetite.

Smash buildings and defeat the military to gain XP. Each level brings a choice of upgrades. Survive rival kaiju at 3:00, 6:00 and 10:00, then defeat the final boss to win.

WASD move · Mouse aim · Left click claws · Hold right click breath · Space evade · Q / E chosen abilities · Esc pause · M sound
On touch screens: left stick moves, right stick aims, and the action buttons attack.
